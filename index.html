Six things Frank does before lunch

Frank is the paralegal in this workspace. He drafts, reads, files and remembers — you keep the judgement and the signature.

Draft the agreement

Founders, shareholders, share purchase, investment, supply, distribution, lease, NDA. Israeli law by default, governing law on request.

Read what came in

Drop a contract and get the parties, the obligations, the dates that bite, and the clauses that should be there but aren't.

Keep the matter moving

Every document becomes a matter with tasks, deadlines and a client file. Export the dates straight to your calendar.

Learn your clients

Feed Frank the file and he builds the picture: who they are, what's been done, what's still owed, and where the exposure sits.

Write the business plan

Full תוכנית עסקית in the Investment Authority format — thirteen sections, five-year P&L, cash flow, NPV. Tkouma and manufacturing tracks.

Hand you the file

Word and PDF out, edit in place before either. Nothing leaves the browser except the model call.
